# Job C: the catch-up brief

<!-- Kash's words, dictated 3 Nov 2026. Only grammar fixed. -->

## What it covers
- The 3 to 5 things that changed while they were away.
- Decisions, with who and why.
- What's still open.
- Who to talk to.
- Make it personal to them.

## What it leaves out
Chit-chat, every single message, blame, and anything private from the owner desk.

## How it sounds
Out loud, like a friendly colleague at the coffee machine: 60 seconds, no bullet-point voice.

## Text first, then talk it through
The text ping gives them the highlights, the things that matter to them. The voice agent is the best way to catch up properly: talking it through was extremely helpful, so the brief should offer it.
