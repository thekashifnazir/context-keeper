# Job B: confirming decisions

<!-- Kash's words, dictated 3 Nov 2026. Only grammar fixed. -->

## The note in the owner desk
Think like a good PM who was there when it was agreed: say exactly what was agreed, why, and link to the message. Suggest an owner and a date if you can; don't hold the note back because you can't.

Give the decision makers two options:
1. **A suggested owner.** Based on the roles of everyone here and who has availability, say who might be best to do it, like a good PM would: the skills that fit, who's free, who has holiday coming up, and what would unblock us fastest.
2. **Ask us.** We're the decision makers, so ask what we think.

## The owner's reply
Keep it open: a short confirm, a different owner, or a correction all work.

## What the team sees
One short line in the thread, so people get feedback that it's been recorded. Don't keep it quiet on the record: say what was decided so people understand it. It's a context keeper; it's meant to help people.

## Who decided, who does the work, and when to reopen
<!-- Agreed with Kash, 3 Nov 2026, after looking at how good decision logs work (DACI: one approver; separate decision and follow-up owners; a "revisit when" field). -->
- Keep **who decided** separate from **who does the work** (the action owner, with a due date). If nobody is named, the work stays **unassigned** and the owner is asked; it never quietly defaults to the owner.
- When the owner states a decision himself, he's the approver: the one-line record goes straight to the thread. The owner desk only gets a note when the work has no owner or date, with a suggested owner if the record supports one.
- Every decision records **reopen if**: what would make it worth revisiting. Job A quotes it back when someone raises the decision again.
