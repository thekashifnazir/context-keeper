# Ledgerly: customer research summary

Owner: Kash. Interviews: 1 to 25 Sep 2026. Last updated Mon 28 Sep 2026.

## §1 Method
- **12 agency interviews** of 30 minutes each, with owners or finance leads at agencies of 2 to 30 people.
- **No survey.** People who answer surveys already want the thing, so the answers skew (decision D4).

## §2 What we heard
- 10 of 12 chase late payments by hand, and it's the most-hated admin task. (This led to the reminders in product.md §2.)
- 7 of 12 named card fees on large invoices as a pain. (This led to GoCardless in product.md §3.)
- 9 of 12 use Xero; 2 use QuickBooks; 1 uses a spreadsheet.
- None asked for a mobile app.
- Their tools cost between £12 and £28 per user per month today.
